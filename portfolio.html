Services

I'm a visual storyteller, who crafts design that hold attention.

  • Motion

    In Premiere Pro, and After Effects I handle the full edit: pacing, colour grading, audio mixing, and final delivery. With my knowledge of Motion Graphics and Video Editing I've turned last-minute briefs into polished videos within quickly, I can simplify a complex concepts into engaging explainers (blockchain tutorial). I understand how pacing, hooks, and sound design hold attention and drive results.

  • Digital Marketing

    I create content that stops the scroll, for Digital Marketing Design, Socials Media and paid Ads. I've designed paid and organic social assets for premium brands (Enterprise, Pet Co, and Pokémon), working across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and YouTube. I move between static ads, carousels, and short-form video, always with a platform-native mindset. I've iterated based on performance data and delivered multiple creative variations for A/B testing. Motion is my edge, but I'm just as comfortable with high-performing static creative.

  • Print Projects

    I have a strong eye for typography, layout, and composition, the fundamentals that make print work standout. I've designed event posters, awards show materials, brochures, decks and signage, always with print production in mind: bleed, crop marks, CMYK, and supplier-ready files. With my great knowledge of Graphic Design's core principles I've built brand guidelines and design systems from scratch, ensuring consistency across every touchpoint. Whether it's a one-off poster or a full campaign rollout, I bring the same level of craft and attention to detail.

  • UX / HTML

    I have a solid foundation in UX/UI principles, gained through a dedicated UX Designer role at World Wide Generation and hands-on experience leading website rebrands. I approach digital products with a user-first mindset, focusing on clear information hierarchy, intuitive navigation, and seamless interaction flows. I've applied UX thinking to website redesigns, ensuring the customer journey is logical, accessible, and conversion-driven. In Figma, I prototype and test layouts that balance visual appeal with usability. I understand that great design isn't just about how something looks, it's about how it works for the people using it. I've built this website with my understanding of HTML and CSS.
