Recon Sales campaign

Breakdown of the client brief for a poster Best Gay Life's Recon Sale:

Primary Objectives:
The primary objective is to create a promotional posters. The poster will be displayed as a banner on Social Media platforms, bus stops and BGL's website.

Design Considerations:
The design should be eye-catching and visually appealing to attract attention. Incorporation of vibrant colours, LGBTQ+ pride symbols, and inclusive imagery to resonate with the target audience.

Promotional Poster:
The promotional poster for the sale should clearly communicate the details of the promotion, including discounts, sale duration, and any other relevant information. It should maintain visual coherence with the rest of the design elements and reflect the brand identity of Best Gay Life.

Review and Feedback:
Regular communication with the client to ensure the design aligns with their vision and meets their expectations. Incorporation of any feedback or revisions to refine the design and ensure client satisfaction.
