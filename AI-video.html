AI Product video

Objective:

This video is to highlight Roxhill's usage of AI. It's supposed to emphasize that AI is used as a gimmick by many companies, but not with Roxhill. We use AI to help your PR work flow.

Tools used for project

  • 10%
    Filming: Sony ZV-E10 II Camera
  • 50%
    Abode Premiere pro
  • 30%
    Abode After Effects
  • 10%
    Abode Illustrator

Process:

The video raises awareness of Roxhill's new AI feature. I filmed it using a Sony ZV-II blogging camera with simple studio front and side lighting. Since I couldn't hide the wall light or wall line while filming, I had to remove it in post-production.

I used a combination of methods to remove the lamp on the wall and correct the video's lighting to make it look seamless. I think the end result is impressive.

Final video

Final Thoughts:

It raised awareness of Roxhill's AI feature and, if I say so myself, was very well presented. The video was used on social media, in email campaigns, and on our site.
